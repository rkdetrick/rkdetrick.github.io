About

Full bio →

I am a researcher, educator, and strategist dedicated to bridging learning theory and technological innovation. As a PhD candidate at Old Dominion University, I investigate how artificial intelligence shapes student self-regulation — and how we can design learning experiences that amplify human potential rather than replace it. I bring nearly a decade of industry experience supporting military-connected learners, and apply that same pragmatism to my academic work.
